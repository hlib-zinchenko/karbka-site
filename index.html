Carve it into memory.

Flashcards you swipe to learn, on your phone, tablet or computer, even offline.

Open Karbka How to install

Swipe right if you know it

Pick the decks and how many cards. Missed cards come back until you know them all.

  1. Choose the decks, the side to show first and the order

  2. One swipe a card, with undo if your thumb slips

  3. See what you knew on the first try, then retry the misses

Six levels, and no schedule

Know moves a card up one level. Don’t know moves it down one. You choose what to study next.

  1. New
  2. Weak
  3. Learning
  4. Confident
  5. Strong
  6. Mastered

Every card, read aloud

Tap the speaker and your device reads the card in its own voices, with no internet. Each collection keeps its languages, with a voice for each.

Write a deck in a spreadsheet

Type front, back, tags and notes in Excel or Google Sheets, then import the CSV. Comma, tab or semicolon all work.

Your cards stay on your device

No account
Open it and start. There is nothing to sign up for.
No server
Cards live in this device’s storage and are never sent anywhere.
Backups you keep
Each device keeps its own cards. Export the whole library, progress included, to move it to another phone, tablet or computer.
Works offline
On a plane or in the metro, it runs from the copy on your device.

Install it from the browser

No App Store and no Play Store. Karbka is a web app for phones, tablets and computers, and installs right from the browser.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open app.karbka.com in Safari
  2. Tap Share
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen

The home-screen app keeps its own storage, apart from Safari. Install it first, then add your cards.

Android

  1. Open app.karbka.com in Chrome
  2. Tap Install app

Phones and tablets alike.

Computer

  1. Open app.karbka.com in Chrome, Edge or Safari on a Mac
  2. In Chrome or Edge, click Install in the address bar. In Safari, choose File, then Add to Dock

Like on iPhone, the Dock app keeps its own storage apart from Safari, so add it first. Or just keep it in a browser tab. Drag cards with the mouse, or press → if you know a card, ← if you don’t, and Space to flip.

Закарбуй у памʼяті.

Karbka comes from карб, the Ukrainian word for a notch cut into a tally stick so you’d remember.

I built it for my partner, who couldn’t find a flashcard app that fit how she studies.
